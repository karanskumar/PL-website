· The Greenfield Convergence Methodology

We don't predict the market.
We find where it's already converging.

The Greenfield Convergence Methodology identifies growth corridors before prices move — using three compounding forces that, when they align, reliably produce sustained capital growth and rental demand.

We map the intersection of closing affordability gaps, forced gentrification, and cost inflation. Where all three converge, supply constraints drive price acceleration.

Every corridor in our supply network has passed all four screens. None of our stock is sourced on speculation.

01

Affordability Convergence

Where median prices meet household income growth. The intersection that drives sustained demand without speculative risk.

02

Infrastructure Catalysts

Government-backed corridors with committed capital in transport, health, and education. The spending that always precedes price movement.

03

Demographic Acceleration

Population growth running above the national average. The leading indicator of rental demand and long-term capital appreciation.

04

Supply Constraints

Limited land release, low vacancy rates, and restrictive zoning. Where scarcity meets demand, prices move. We only work here.

So you're an aggregator?

In structure, yes — we sit behind the adviser the way an aggregator sits behind a broker. But the comparison only goes so far. An aggregator provides access to lenders. PropLaunch provides access to exclusive supply, a vetted research pipeline, and an end-to-end fulfilment operation. The economics are aligned the same way: we earn when you settle.

Why pay when Basecamp is free?

Basecamp gives you the supply relationship. Launchpad and Orbit give you the machine to convert it — a business operating system, AI workflows, paid acquisition, and a direct acquisitions desk. If you're already running at volume and want to systemise, the investment has a clear ROI. If you're starting out, Basecamp is the right entry point.

What if stock dries up?

It's the right question, and the answer is in the supply structure. PropLaunch controls supply at the source — build partners who build exclusively through us, land optioned before it reaches market, and in-house developments. We're not a portal that aggregates whatever developers list. If we don't have the right stock, we say so rather than pushing product that doesn't pass the screen.
