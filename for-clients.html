— For clients of our partners

Your adviser chose us for a reason.

If your buyer's agent, accountant or financial planner sources property through PropLaunch, this page explains what that means for you — and why it's a mark of quality, not a middleman.

— What PropLaunch is

The infrastructure behind the advice.

PropLaunch is Australia's leading wholesale property group. We don't advise you and we don't sell to you — we supply and support the professional who does. Every property we release has passed our Greenfield Convergence Methodology before it reaches your adviser's desk.

01
Researched, not listed

Every property passes the Greenfield Convergence Methodology — our proprietary framework that screens for affordability gaps, infrastructure catalysts, demographic tailwinds and replacement-cost economics. We do not list properties. We select them.

02
Vetted, exclusive builders

Our build partners are vetted on financials, track record and construction quality before we secure any allocation. We don't use whoever's available — we work with a closed network of proven operators across QLD, VIC, NSW, WA and SA.

03
Independently inspected

Every build is independently inspected at key milestones — slab, frame, lockup, practical completion. Reports are provided to your adviser. We do not rely on builder self-reporting at any stage of construction.

04
Managed to the keys

From contract execution through builder liaison, finance milestone management and practical completion — PropLaunch manages the process end-to-end so your adviser can focus on advice, not administration.

— Why you can trust the model

Our incentives point the same way as yours.

PropLaunch charges its partners — the buyer's agents and advisers in our network — not you. We are not a developer marketing arm, we are not a referral network, and we do not receive commissions from builders on a per-sale basis. Our commercial model depends entirely on our partners continuing to use us, which means it depends entirely on the quality of what we supply.

And we never sell direct. If you have found this page without a professional introduction, you cannot buy through PropLaunch — that boundary is structural, not a sales tactic. It protects the integrity of every deal in our pipeline.

Buying wholesale works in your favour — you buy closer to inception, before display-village costs, marketing levies and retail margins are stacked on the price. Your adviser's access to PropLaunch is access to product that is structurally closer to cost.
