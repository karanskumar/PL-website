— Further reading

Forthcoming papers

The research pipeline — each paper addresses a distinct pillar of the PropLaunch investment methodology.

  • The Affordability Convergence Gap

    An empirical analysis of the divergence between median dwelling prices and household income across Australian capital cities and growth corridors — and why greenfield markets consistently absorb displaced demand from adjacent premium suburbs.

    Forthcoming · Q3 2026

  • Replacement Cost Economics

    Why the cost of building a new dwelling has become the primary price floor in Australia's residential market — examining material costs, labour constraints, and the structural gap between replacement cost and land-plus-build market pricing.

    Forthcoming · Q3 2026

  • Reading Infrastructure Catalysts

    A framework for interpreting announced government infrastructure spend — roads, rail, schools, hospitals — as leading indicators of residential price movement, with case studies across PropLaunch's five-state portfolio.

    Forthcoming · Q4 2026

  • Demographic Acceleration vs Organic Gentrification

    Distinguishing between population-driven demand growth — the kind that compounds — and sentiment-driven gentrification, which peaks and reverts. How PropLaunch identifies the former before the market prices it in.

    Forthcoming · Q4 2026
